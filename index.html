Reload balance

Collect payment first, then credit the selected student's account.

$1 equals 1 point. Reload buttons record the credit, but do not charge a bank card or collect money.

Quick instructions

Student ID: Loads the name and available points instantly from the saved roster.
Snack barcode: Automatically sends a purchase to the database. A successful purchase deducts 1 or 2 points and stock.
Another student ID: Switches to the next customer.
Low balance: Purchase is blocked instead of allowing negative points.
An internet connection is required to confirm transactions. Local barcode lookup is instant; database confirmation speed depends on the connection.

How balances work

All students begin with 0 points. Add 5 or 10 points when they pay. You can manually increase or decrease points for corrections, and every change is saved to transaction history.

Deactivation hides a student from checkout but preserves their records.

Barcode scanner tip

A Honeywell Hyperion 1300g scanner acts like a keyboard. Click a barcode input and scan. It should enter the full barcode followed by Enter. Keep leading zeroes, and never store barcode IDs as numbers.

To update stock or points later, enter the same barcode and save again.
